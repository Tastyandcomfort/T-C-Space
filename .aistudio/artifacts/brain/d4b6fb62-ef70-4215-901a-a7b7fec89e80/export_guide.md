# Space Hub — Download & Export Guide

Here is where the export options and project files are located on your screen:

---

## 1. How to Download the Complete ZIP

Look at the **top right corner** of your Google AI Studio window (to the right of the **Publish** button):

1. Click the **Gear icon (⚙️)** (Settings).
2. In the menu that opens, click **"Export as ZIP"** (or **"Download ZIP"**).
3. Your browser will download the complete Android project ZIP with all Kotlin code, Gradle scripts, vector maps, and assets.

---

## 2. How to Browse & Download Individual Code Files

Look right above this panel at the top of the center workspace:

You will see three icons:
- **`👁` (Eye)**: Live interactive preview
- **`<>` (Code brackets)**: **Click this `<>` icon!** It will open the full File Explorer and Code Editor, displaying all source files:
  - `app/src/main/java/com/example/` (MainActivity, ViewModel, Screens, Engine)
  - `app/src/main/res/` (Icons, Drawables, Typography, Themes)
  - `SpaceHub-Android.zip` (Pre-packaged archive file)

---

## 3. How to Push Directly to GitHub

1. Click the **Gear icon (⚙️)** in the top right corner.
2. Select **"Push to GitHub"**.
3. Sign in to your GitHub account (`tastyandcomfort`).
4. Choose your repository (or create a new one, e.g. `T-C-Space-Hub-Android`) and click **Push**.

---

## 4. Where the Pre-Packaged ZIP is Saved

A standalone `.zip` archive has also been generated in the workspace root:
- **Path**: `/SpaceHub-Android.zip` and `/public/SpaceHub-Android.zip`
- Switch to the **`<>` (Code)** view above to browse and download it directly.
